ABOUT THE PROJECT

Calculus you can see.

Triple integration is used to calculate volumes of three-dimensional regions. VECTORFLOW 3D combines mathematical computation with interactive visualization to help students understand how integration limits define a solid and how its volume is accumulated.

Objectives

  • Calculate volumes numerically
  • Visualize bounded 3D regions
  • Show integration limits and steps
  • Build intuition for multivariable calculus

Applications

  • Engineering and construction
  • Tank and container capacity
  • Manufacturing and modeling
